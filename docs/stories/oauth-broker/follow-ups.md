## From story oauth-token-endpoint (2026-10-10)

Design context: ADR-033 "Token endpoint, sessions, refresh and id_token (story 3)".

- (a) **Trusted-proxy hard gate confirmed (RESOLVED in the server by story oauth-trusted-proxy-client-ip; operator verification pending).** Item 1 also covers the new per-IP buckets

- (c) **No `display_name` for OAuth devices** (O5, accepted by the oracle): a new device created at
